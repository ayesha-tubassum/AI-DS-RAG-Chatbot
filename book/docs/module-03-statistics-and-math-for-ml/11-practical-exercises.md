# Module 03 Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Central Tendency
For `[12, 15, 15, 18, 20, 25, 30]`, calculate mean, median, and mode.

## Exercise 2 — Spread
For the same data, calculate range, variance, and standard deviation. Explain the standard deviation.

## Exercise 3 — NumPy Summary
Create an array of at least 20 values and generate a statistical summary.

## Exercise 4 — Probability
For a fair die, calculate the probability of rolling 6, an even number, and a number greater than 4.

## Exercise 5 — Distribution Selection
Identify whether Normal, Binomial, or Poisson could reasonably model:
1. Successes in 20 binary trials.
2. Calls arriving during an hour.
3. An approximately bell-shaped measurement.

## Exercise 6 — Hypotheses
Write H₀ and H₁ for a company testing whether training changes average employee performance.

## Exercise 7 — Correlation
Create two related numerical variables, calculate Pearson correlation, and plot them.

## Exercise 8 — Correlation Warning
Give three reasons correlation does not prove causation.

## Exercise 9 — Vectors
Create two NumPy vectors and calculate sum, difference, scalar multiplication, and dot product.

## Exercise 10 — Matrices
Create compatible matrices and calculate their product using `@`.

## Exercise 11 — MSE
Calculate MSE for:
```text
Actual:    [10, 20, 30, 40]
Predicted: [12, 18, 33, 35]
```

## Exercise 12 — Gradient
For `f(x)=x²`, find the derivative and explain why gradient descent moves toward the minimum.

## Challenge
Choose a real dataset and produce descriptive statistics, a probability analysis, correlation study, a hypothesis-testing question, and a short explanation of how the findings support later ML work.
