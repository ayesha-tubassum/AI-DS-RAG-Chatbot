# Module 03 Review and Completion Checklist

## Module Overview
This module builds the mathematical and statistical foundation required for later machine learning.

Topics covered:
- Descriptive Statistics
- Probability and Distributions
- Hypothesis Testing
- Covariance and Correlation
- Linear Algebra Basics
- Cost Functions and Gradients
- Statistics projects

## Final Review Questions
1. Why are descriptive statistics useful?
2. What is the difference between mean and median?
3. What does standard deviation tell us?
4. What is a probability distribution?
5. When is Binomial distribution appropriate?
6. What is the role of H₀?
7. What does a p-value tell us?
8. Why does correlation not prove causation?
9. What are vectors and matrices used for in ML?
10. Why do models need loss or cost functions?
11. What does a gradient represent?
12. What is the basic idea of gradient descent?

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] Calculate mean, median, and mode.
- [ ] Explain variance and standard deviation.
- [ ] Distinguish population and sample.
- [ ] Explain basic probability.
- [ ] Recognize Normal, Binomial, and Poisson distributions.
- [ ] Define H₀ and H₁.
- [ ] Explain p-values correctly.
- [ ] Distinguish statistical from practical significance.
- [ ] Calculate covariance and correlation.
- [ ] Explain why correlation does not prove causation.
- [ ] Work with vectors and matrices in NumPy.
- [ ] Explain dot products and matrix multiplication.
- [ ] Explain loss/cost functions.
- [ ] Explain gradients and gradient descent.
- [ ] Complete at least one statistics project.
