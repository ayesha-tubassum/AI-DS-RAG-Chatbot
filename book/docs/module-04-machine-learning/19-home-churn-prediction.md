# Home Project — Churn Prediction

## Goal
Predict whether a customer is likely to leave a service.

## Suggested Features
Tenure, monthly charges, contract type, support interactions, payment method, and usage behavior.

## Tasks
1. Explore and clean the data.
2. Encode categorical variables.
3. Scale where appropriate.
4. Split into train/test sets.
5. Train at least three classifiers.
6. Compare accuracy, precision, recall, and F1.
7. Analyze important features.
8. Write business recommendations.

## Questions
- Which customers appear most at risk?
- Which metric matters most?
- What action could follow a high-risk prediction?

## Deliverables
Notebook, model comparison, confusion matrix, feature analysis, and recommendation report.


## Syllabus Focus

Predict customer attrition using historical customer data.
